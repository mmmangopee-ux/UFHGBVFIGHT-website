Provide immediate support and resources to callers in crisis.

Responsibilities:

  • Answer crisis calls with empathy
  • Provide information about resources
  • Document calls appropriately
  • Follow safety protocols
Remote Training Provided 4-hour shifts

Time Commitment: 8+ hours per month
