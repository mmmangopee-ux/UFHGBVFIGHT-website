About GBV Safe Corner

Creating safe, anonymous digital spaces for survivors of gender-based violence

Our Story

GBV Safe Corner was born from a critical need for safe, accessible, and anonymous support for survivors of gender-based violence in South Africa. Founded in 2023, our platform emerged from community conversations with survivors, support workers, and advocates who identified gaps in existing support systems.

Our Mission

To create a trusted, anonymous digital sanctuary where survivors of gender-based violence can safely access support, share experiences, and find community without fear of judgment, stigma, or identification. We believe everyone deserves access to help and healing on their own terms.

Our Journey

2023 - Foundation

Identified the critical need for anonymous GBV support through community research and survivor interviews. Conducted extensive user research to understand the unique challenges faced by survivors in South Africa.

2024 - Platform Launch

Launched the first version of GBV Safe Corner with basic reporting and resource directory features. Partnered with 5 local support organizations to verify our initial service directory.

2024 - Expansion

Added multi-language support, safety planning tools, and community features based on user feedback. Expanded our verified support network to include 13+ organizations across South Africa.

Ongoing - Growth

Continually improving based on survivor input and expanding our verified support network. Developing new features and partnerships to better serve our community.

What We Offer

🛡️ Anonymous Reporting System

Safe, confidential incident reporting with complete privacy protection and no data tracking. Share your experience without fear of identification.

✅ Verified Support Directory

Carefully vetted organizations and professionals ensuring user safety and trust. Every service is verified for quality and reliability.

💬 Community Story Sharing

Safe spaces to share experiences and find peer support from those who understand. Connect with others who have walked similar paths.

🎓 Educational Resources

Comprehensive information about GBV prevention, rights, healing, and support options. Learn about your rights and available resources.

📅 Event Organization Tools

Platform to mobilize awareness campaigns, support events, and community gatherings. Build momentum for change in your community.

🤝 Peer Support Community

Anonymous Q&A and community advice sharing in a moderated, safe environment. Get support from people who truly understand.
