💡 Shelter Preparation Tips

  • Bring essential documents (ID, medical cards, bank cards)
  • Pack medications and basic necessities
  • Bring important items for children (favorite toys, comfort items)
  • Have a list of important phone numbers
  • Shelters provide basic necessities - don't worry about packing everything
